Conferences

Nine papers, four countries

Presenting research from Huddersfield to Istanbul, Cappadocia, Abu Dhabi and Melbourne, supported by £5,860 of competitively won travel funding.

Open the photo gallery
  • September 2026, United Kingdom

    ICMIAM 2026, University of Huddersfield

    7th International Conference on Maintenance and Intelligent Asset Management, 1 to 3 September. Two papers: Multi-fuel combustion analysis of ammonia, gasoline and hydrogen in a spark-ignition engine, and Comparative performance and emissions analysis of ammonia, diesel and hydrogen in a compression-ignition engine.

  • May 2026, Turkey

    ICES 2026, Yildiz Technical University, Istanbul

    Two papers: Comparative performance and emissions analysis of diesel, hydrogen–diesel and methane–diesel combustion, and Assessment of ammonia substitution in compression-ignition engines.

  • December 2025, Australia

    ICMIAM 2025, Federation University, Berwick

    6th International Conference on Maintenance and Intelligent Asset Management, 10 to 12 December. Two papers: Bridging data and physics: a SINDy-driven framework for modelling hydrogen–diesel dual-fuel combustion, and Performance evaluation of single-hidden-layer artificial neural networks for emissions prediction in hydrogen-enriched diesel engines. Travel supported by £5,060 of departmental research and conference funding.

  • 2024, Turkey

    ICEESEN 2024, Cappadocia University

    Effects of hydrogen energy share on the performance of a compression-ignition engine running at high load. Supported by an £800 conference travel grant.

  • 2024, United Arab Emirates

    ICASF 2024, Abu Dhabi University

    Development of a novel and sustainable multi-axis driven reconfigurable wheelchair (MA-DRW) for older adults and individuals with mobility impairment, sharing the EPSRC IAA project with an international, multidisciplinary audience.

  • 2023, United Kingdom

    Unified Conference 2023, University of Huddersfield

    Effects of hydrogen–diesel blend on performance characteristics of a compression-ignition engine, the first public outing of my doctoral research.

Why conferences matter to me

Every trip sharpened the research: questions from combustion specialists in Cappadocia, clinicians and designers in Abu Dhabi, and data-driven modellers in Australia all fed into the papers that followed. Presenting across four countries also built a network I keep in touch with, and a real love of travel.

Funding secured for dissemination

  • £5,060: Departmental Research and Conference Funding (ICMIAM 2025, Australia).
  • £800: Conference Travel Grant (ICEESEN 2024, Turkey).
