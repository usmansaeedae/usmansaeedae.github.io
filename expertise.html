Expertise & software

The technical toolkit

Deep expertise in thermofluids and simulation, built across a PhD, a funded design project and three years of teaching — every area below is evidenced in published or delivered work.

Part A

Technical expertise

Hydrogen & dual-fuel combustion

Energy share, injection strategy and geometry effects for H2–diesel engines — six Q1 papers.

Compression-ignition engines

In-cylinder processes, performance and emissions of CI engines, validated against JCB test-engine data.

Computational fluid dynamics

3D reacting-flow CFD with detailed chemistry in Fluent and Forte; aerodynamics CFD from BEng and teaching practicals.

1D engine & systems simulation

Whole-engine modelling in Ricardo WAVE, extended with a custom C++ fuel-property module for hydrogen blends.

Combustion chemistry

Detailed and reduced kinetic mechanisms applied through CHEMKIN-Pro and CFD-coupled chemistry.

Machine-learning surrogates

SINDy and ANN surrogate modelling of engine response — R² 0.81–0.99 with ~108× evaluation speed-up.

Multi-objective optimisation

NSGA-II design-space exploration with TOPSIS multi-criteria decision-making for balanced engine designs.

Engineering design & CAD

Parts, assemblies and drawings in SolidWorks; parametric geometry in SpaceClaim and DesignModeler; concept-to-prototype delivery.

FEA & multibody dynamics

Structural validation via finite element analysis and stability/kinematic simulation in MSC ADAMS on the NHS wheelchair project.

Experimental engine testing

Hands-on testing and data acquisition on a JCB diesel engine, providing the validation base for all modelling.

Thermodynamics & heat transfer

From absorption-refrigeration desorber analysis to thermal-driven dehumidification and pyrolysis multiphysics reviews.

Verification & validation

Grid and model sensitivity practice, validation against measurement, and honest reporting of uncertainty and limitations.
