9.2Impact factor

Sparse identification with multi-objective optimisation and multi-criteria decision-making for predicting hydrogen–diesel dual-fuel engine performance

International Journal of Hydrogen Energy2026Research article

Couples SINDy surrogate models with NSGA-II optimisation and TOPSIS decision-making for hydrogen–diesel engines, reaching R² of 0.81–0.99 against CFD while cutting per-point evaluation time from ~87 s to ~4.5×10−7 s.

Contribution: makes multi-objective engine optimisation computationally practical through interpretable, physics-informed surrogates.

Investigating the effects of stroke-to-bore ratio on the performance of hydrogen-fuelled diesel engines

Energy Conversion and Management: X2026Research article

A simulation-based study isolating how stroke-to-bore ratio — a fundamental geometric lever — shapes the performance of hydrogen-fuelled diesel engines.
