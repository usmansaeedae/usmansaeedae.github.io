Durham postdoc, 2026 to present

Digital models of the MAN D2862 hydrogen–diesel marine engine

Overview: machine-learning models of a V12 dual-fuel marine engine built from the manufacturer's published performance data, with an interactive advisor app for diesel use, hydrogen use, efficiency and CO2.

  • Methods: curve digitisation and extension to more than 1,000 points, seven model families compared, TOPSIS selection, support vector regression and neural networks tested on a hidden block of engine speeds.
  • Outcomes: the D2862 Dual-Fuel Advisor app, with a reliability label on every result; journal paper in preparation.

Doctoral research · 2023–2026

Hydrogen–diesel dual-fuel engine research platform

Overview: the complete modelling-and-experiment platform behind my PhD — validated 1D and 3D engine models, machine-learning surrogates and a multi-objective optimisation framework for hydrogen–diesel compression-ignition engines.

  • Objectives: quantify how energy share, injection technique and geometry shape performance and emissions; make optimisation computationally practical.
  • Methods: Ricardo WAVE (1D), ANSYS Fluent/Forte with detailed chemistry (3D CFD), parametric CAD/meshing, validation against JCB test-engine measurements, SINDy/ANN surrogates, NSGA-II + TOPSIS.
  • Outcomes: six Q1 journal papers on hydrogen combustion; surrogates with R² 0.81–0.99 at ~108× speed-up; PhD awarded in 2026.
  • Skills: combustion modelling, V&V discipline, scientific software development, technical writing.

EPSRC IAA · Barts Life Sciences (NHS) · Nov 2023 – Jul 2024

Multi-axis driven reconfigurable wheelchair (MA-DRW)

Overview: a funded assistive-technology project developing a novel reconfigurable wheelchair for older adults and individuals with mobility impairment, delivered with an NHS-linked partner.

  • My role: led CAD modelling in SolidWorks — detailed part and assembly models carrying the design from concept to physical prototype with full external sign-off.
  • Analysis: validated structural integrity through finite element analysis, resolving high-stress regions; simulated stability and kinematics in MSC ADAMS under representative loading.
  • Outcomes: funded work packages delivered on time; design presented at ICASF 2024, Abu Dhabi, to an international multidisciplinary audience.
  • Skills: full design–build cycle, requirements capture, stakeholder communication with clinical partners.

Methodology · published IJHE 2026

SINDy surrogate & optimisation framework

Overview: a physics-informed framework that learns compact, interpretable equations from CFD data (sparse identification of nonlinear dynamics), then drives NSGA-II multi-objective optimisation with TOPSIS decision-making.

  • Objectives: replace prohibitive per-point CFD cost in design studies without losing physical interpretability.
  • Results: R² of 0.81–0.99 against CFD; evaluation time reduced from ~87 s to ~4.5×10−7 s per point.
  • Outcomes: journal paper in IJHE (2026) and a conference paper at ICMIAM 2025, Australia.

Scientific software · PhD toolchain

Custom C++ fuel-property module for Ricardo WAVE

Overview: standard 1D fuel libraries did not represent hydrogen–diesel blends the way the research required — so I developed a custom C++ fuel-property module extending Ricardo WAVE for accurate dual-fuel system simulation.

  • Objectives: accurate thermophysical representation of hydrogen–diesel operation within the 1D modelling chain.
  • Outcomes: enabled the validated WAVE models underpinning the PhD's operating-space studies.
  • Skills: C++, solver integration, verification against reference data.

Machine learning · ICMIAM 2025

ANN emissions prediction for hydrogen-enriched engines

Overview: performance evaluation of single-hidden-layer artificial neural networks for emissions prediction in hydrogen-enriched diesel engines — establishing how far deliberately simple architectures can go.

  • Outcomes: presented at ICMIAM 2025, Federation University, Australia; complements the SINDy work as the black-box counterpart in the surrogate toolbox.
