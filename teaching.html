Teaching & student education

Research-led teaching that students can use

Three academic years of undergraduate teaching at the University of Huddersfield — tutorials, simulation practicals, wind-tunnel laboratories and project supervision, grounded in live research.

Philosophy

Active, honest, inclusive

I structure practical teaching around active, problem-based learning: students move from worked examples to independent model set-up, solution and — crucially — interpretation of results. Software buttons are easy; judgement is the skill.

Because my teaching sits on a live research base, students see how the tools they are learning are used at the frontier — the same CFD workflows that appear in my papers appear, scaled appropriately, in their laboratories.

And having arrived in the UK as an international student myself, I teach with a first-hand understanding of diverse cohorts: adapting explanations to very different starting points, and treating clear feedback as part of the teaching, not an add-on.

Experience

Teaching Assistant, 2023–2026

  • Delivered tutorial and laboratory teaching to more than 60 undergraduates across three academic years, including CFD simulation practicals and wind-tunnel laboratory sessions — with consistently positive student feedback for clarity and engagement.
  • Developed simulation-based digital learning materials from scratch, filling a gap in curriculum provision and giving students structured, hands-on practice with industry-standard software.
  • Marked coursework and contributed to internal moderation, building assessment and feedback practice consistent with UK HE standards.
  • Taught and supported a large, diverse and international cohort.

Supervision

Six projects, brief to submission

I co-supervised six undergraduate and postgraduate design and analysis projects from brief to final submission — providing structured one-to-one guidance on CAD methodology, CFD and complex-systems modelling, and contributing written and verbal feedback and assessment of final submissions.

Laboratories

Wind-tunnel experimental sessions and computer-laboratory CFD practicals — the pairing that lets students test simulation against measurement, the same discipline my research runs on.
